                                            with stereotypes that may influence the decision-making process. Previous research
                                            shows that referees’ decisions in men’s professional sports are often biased in favor of
                                            racial and nationalistic in-groups. This study examined if similar biases exist in women’s
                                            professional sports. Additionally, this study analyzed the potential influence of the gender
                                            composition of referee teams on rapid decisions. We gathered data on referee foul
                                            calls in women’s professional basketball in Spain, 2014–2019 and defined important
