# Zotero setup for the WMI bibliography

The official Zotero 10.0.6 macOS app is installed at `~/Applications/Zotero.app`. The official installer is in `~/Downloads/WMI-next-meeting/Zotero-macOS.dmg`. Ten source records have been imported into Zotero's `wmi_sources` collection. Five available PDFs are in `~/Downloads/WMI-next-meeting/articles/`.

## Finish on your Mac

1. Open Zotero from `~/Applications/Zotero.app`. Check the ten imported records in the `wmi_sources` collection against their publisher pages.
2. Create or sign in to an account at [zotero.org](https://www.zotero.org/user/register) if you want library sync. Account creation requires your own email/password and verification.
3. Attach the five downloaded PDFs to their matching Zotero records. Use your library or publisher access for the other five where available.
4. Install the [official Zotero Connector for Chrome](https://www.zotero.org/download/), then use it to save a paper from Google Scholar or a publisher page. Browser extension installation needs your browser approval.
5. For each paper, attach an available full text legally through the publisher, library, or Zotero’s PDF retrieval. Add notes with page numbers; highlight claims and methods separately. Do not treat the preliminary annotations as full-paper notes.
6. Zotero bundles Word/LibreOffice plugins and installs them on first launch when supported. In Google Docs, sign into Zotero Connector and look for the Zotero menu. Test one in-text citation and bibliography in a scratch document.
7. Choose the citation style with Joe or Indigo. APA 7 is a reasonable working format, but the official requirement has not been provided.
