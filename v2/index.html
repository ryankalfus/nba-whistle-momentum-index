    Synthetic mathematics only

    A high WMI can arise without history dependence.

    Varying foul probabilities can cluster recorded fouls even when every draw is independent. WMI is a retrospective descriptive ratio, not a test of independence.

    Exact formula and uncertainty limits

    L is any counted foul in the previous two possessions; N is any in the next two. M = F × (1 + N). WMI = mean(M | L = 1) / mean(M | L = 0). Windows use global game order, cross periods and truncate at game edges.

    Undefined groups and denominators stay missing. The mean shown conditions on WMI being defined. Simulation quantiles describe generated games; they are not empirical NBA confidence intervals. Probability curves show the baseline; history scenarios additionally change probability after a previous foul.
