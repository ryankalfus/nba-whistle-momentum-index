How to read a game

WMI > 1
More whistle momentum after recent defensive fouls.
WMI ~= 1
Little or no difference in the short-term pattern.
WMI < 1
Less whistle momentum after recent defensive fouls.

Method

One formula, one active metric

WMI uses possession windows. It does not adjust for score, teams, period, or late-game strategy in v1.

WMI = average(M_t where L_t = 1) / average(M_t where L_t = 0)
  • L_t equals 1 when one of the previous two possessions had a counted defensive foul.
  • F_t equals 1 when the current possession had a counted defensive foul.
  • N_t equals 1 when one of the next two possessions had a counted defensive foul.
  • M_t equals F_t + F_t*N_t.

WMI is a pattern metric. It can flag games worth reviewing, but it is not proof of referee intent, bias, or misconduct.
