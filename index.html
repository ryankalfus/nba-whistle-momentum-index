Research validation

Does foul history add information?

The validation study compares the same basketball-context model with and without recent foul history, holding out later seasons and resampling whole games for uncertainty.

Period boundaries and prediction timing were corrected. Reconstructed start-state features include score, clock, team fouls and bonus eligibility. Current-possession outcomes are excluded from predictors.

Results and the expansion decision are documented in the validation report.

Method

One formula, one active metric

WMI uses global possession windows. Its formula is unchanged. It is retrospective because the next-two-possession term uses future events. WMI does not adjust for basketball context; the separate prediction study tests whether history adds information beyond context.

WMI = average(M_t where L_t = 1) / average(M_t where L_t = 0)
  • L_t equals 1 when one of the previous two possessions had a counted defensive foul.
  • F_t equals 1 when the current possession had a counted defensive foul.
  • N_t equals 1 when one of the next two possessions had a counted defensive foul.
  • M_t equals F_t + F_t*N_t.
